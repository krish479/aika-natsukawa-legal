# 🦋 Aika Natsukawa — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026

Welcome to Aika Natsukawa! This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you interact with the Aika Natsukawa Discord bot ("Aika", "the Bot", "we", or "our").

Aika is an anime-themed Discord bot inspired by *Yumemiru Danshi wa Genjitsushugisha*, developed and maintained by **XIICOMMANDERIIX**.

We aim to handle information responsibly and collect only what is reasonably necessary to operate the Bot's features.

---

## 1. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the features enabled and how Aika is implemented, the Bot may process the following information:

### Discord Account and Server Information

- Discord user IDs and server IDs.
- Server, channel, and role IDs needed to perform requested actions.
- Usernames and display names when needed to display responses or moderation records.

### Messages and Commands

- Commands and interactions submitted to Aika.
- Message activity or counts if activity-tracking features are enabled.
- Message content temporarily processed to provide a requested feature or enforce anti-spam rules, where applicable.

### Moderation and Security Information

- Spam or mention counts used by anti-abuse systems.
- Moderation events, actions, and relevant timestamps.
- Server configuration and settings needed to operate moderation features.

### Technical Information

Hosting providers, Discord, or other services may process technical information required to operate their systems. The information involved depends on the services actually used.

**Important:** The information above describes possible processing associated with Aika's features. Actual collection and storage depend on the deployed implementation and enabled features.

## 2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed to:

- Respond to commands and interactions.
- Provide activity tracking and leveling features.
- Detect spam and support server moderation.
- Remember server-specific configurations.
- Troubleshoot errors and improve reliability.
- Protect the Bot and its users against abuse.

## 3. Message Content

Aika may need to read or process message events to provide certain functions, such as spam detection or message activity tracking.

Where possible, message processing should be limited to what the relevant feature requires.

Do not assume that messages are never stored: retention depends on the Bot's actual implementation, logs, and hosting configuration.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord and by hosting or other service providers where necessary to operate Aika. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or the security of its services.

This section must be updated if additional integrations or third-party providers receive user information.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable requirements.

Retention periods depend on the Bot's actual storage systems and configuration. We will provide specific retention periods when they have been established.

If you have a question about information associated with your Discord account, contact the developer through Aika's official support channel.

Deletion requests may be subject to technical limitations, legitimate operational needs, and applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information and access credentials against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Aika is a community bot, not a service designed to collect children's personal information.

Users should not submit sensitive personal information through commands or messages. Any concerns about information involving a child should be raised through the official support channel.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Aika depends on Discord and may use hosting or other third-party services.

Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the relevant policies to understand how they handle information.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

You can stop interacting with Aika or ask a server administrator to remove the Bot from a server. This may not automatically delete information already stored in logs or databases.

Contact the developer to raise a privacy question or request.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Aika's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published in this repository. The revision date will be updated when material changes are made.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the official support channel associated with Aika Natsukawa.
